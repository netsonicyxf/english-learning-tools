# Daily Upgrade — 日常升级词单（~110 词，母语者常用、学习者少用的好词）

「学了就能用」的词：替换掉 good/bad/big/sad 这类万金油。

## 段 1（替换万金油形容词）
delightful, dreadful, massive, subtle, brilliant, mediocre, stunning, shabby, cozy, hectic

## 段 2（精确的动作）
grab, stroll, sprint, mutter, yell, whisper, glance, stare, toss, fiddle

## 段 3（情绪与状态）
exhausted, thrilled, anxious, relieved, annoyed, delighted, overwhelmed, content, restless, startled

## 段 4（社交短语）
catch up, drop by, count on, back down, show off, hit it off, break the ice, run into, put off, look forward to

## 段 5（职场邮件升级）
regarding, apologize for the delay, attached is, at your earliest convenience, circling back, per our conversation, touch base, follow up, on leave, out of the loop

## 段 6（表达观点升级——替换 I think）
it seems to me, from my perspective, if you ask me, as far as I know, my take on it, the way I see it, I'd say, correct me if I'm wrong, to be fair, frankly speaking

## 段 7（描述食物与味觉）
savory, bland, crispy, juicy, tender, sour, bitter, rich, refreshing, mouth-watering

## 段 8（描述天气与氛围）
chilly, humid, drizzle, downpour, breezy, gloomy, misty, scorching, overcast, crisp

## 段 9（网络与日常科技用语）
glitch, crash, scroll, notification, update, charge, download, live stream, meme, binge-watch

## 段 10（委婉与礼貌升级——替换 no）
I'm afraid not, that works for me, I'd rather not, let me get back to you, I see where you're coming from, not really my thing, perhaps another time, would you mind, sorry to bother you, no worries

## 段 11（收尾好词）
serendipity, wanderlust, nostalgia, vibe, wholesome, awkward, fulfilling, mundane, spontaneous, hilarious

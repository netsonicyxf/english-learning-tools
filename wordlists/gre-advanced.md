# GRE Advanced — GRE 进阶词单（~120 词，填空/阅读核心难词）

## 段 1（态度与评价）
laudatory, disparaging, ambivalent, indifferent, sycophant, skeptic, cynic, pragmatist, idealist, detractor

## 段 2（言语与表达）
eloquent, garrulous, reticent, terse, verbose, lucid, obfuscate, equivocate, prevaricate, expound

## 段 3（性质与程度）
ephemeral, perpetual, ubiquitous, scarce, copious, meager, superfluous, requisite, salient, negligible

## 段 4（逻辑与论证）
cogent, specious, fallacious, corroborate, undermine, bolster, impugn, vindicate, tenable, irrefutable

## 段 5（性格与气质）
quixotic, mercurial, obdurate, prodigal, pusillanimous, perspicacious, truculent, intransigent, magnanimous, epitome

## 段 6（变化与波动）
vacillate, oscillate, waver, fluctuate, metamorphose, ameliorate, exacerbate, diminish, augment, abate

## 段 7（怀疑与确信）
credulous, incredulous, skeptical, dubious, unequivocal, palpable, incontrovertible, conjecture, surmise, vacuous

## 段 8（隐藏与显露）
candid, forthright, dissemble, clandestine, overt, covert, divulge, conceal, manifest, enigmatic

## 段 9（赞美与谴责）
eulogize, extol, censure, reprove, admonish, berate, acclaim, disparage, lionize, denigrate

## 段 10（学术阅读高频）
paradigm, empirical, deterministic, reductionist, anomaly, discourse, epistemology, heuristic, a priori, dialectic

## 段 11（形近易混——成对记：adjacent/adjourn、aesthetic/ascetic、abstruse/obtuse、censor/censure、compile/comply）
adjacent, adjourn, aesthetic, ascetic, abstruse, obtuse, censor, censure, compile, comply

## 段 12（收尾地狱级）
perfidious, lugubrious, recalcitrant, pellucid, anodyne, nugatory, soporific, evanescent, intractable, ineffable

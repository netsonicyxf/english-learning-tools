# SAT Reading — SAT 阅读词单（~110 词，文学/历史/社科语料高频）

admiration, contempt, indifference, nostalgia, apprehension, exuberant, melancholy, candid, resolute, apprehensive

## 段 2（论述与修辞）
assertion, concession, refute, corroborate, anecdote, rhetoric, irony, satire, juxtapose, underscore

hypothesis, correlate, paradigm, anomaly, scrutiny, plausible, skeptics, consensus, disseminate, empirical

## 段 7（科学说明文高频）
compound, molecule, velocity, generate, transform, demonstrate, conduct, sustain, deviation, residual

## 段 8（文学叙事高频）
foreshadow, allegory, protagonist, antagonist, narrator, motif, imagery, vernacular, dialect, colloquial

## 段 9（双篇对比题态度词）
